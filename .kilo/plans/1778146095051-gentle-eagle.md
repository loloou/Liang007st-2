# Whiteboard Redefinition Plan: AI Creative Workflow Canvas

Inspired by RunningHub RHTV, this plan evolves the current whiteboard from a simple "image container" to a professional "creative workspace" focusing on lineage, workflows, and project management.

## 1. Core Redefinition: The "Lineage" System
In RunningHub, connections show the flow of data. Our canvas will move from free-floating cards to a **Parent-Child Visual Flow**.

### Implementation:
- **Connection Logic**: When a card is created via "Variant", "Inpaint", or "Upscale", the store will track its `parentId`.
- **Visual Lines**: Draw elegant, semi-transparent curved lines (SVG) connecting the `parentId` card to the new card.
- **Workflow Snap**: Automatically position child cards to the right of their parents if space is available.

## 2. The "Magic Input" (Enhanced ChatInputBar)
The input bar will become the "Orchestrator" of the canvas.

### Features:
- **Commands**: Support `/` commands (e.g., `/clear`, `/export`, `/bg-remove`) with an autocomplete popup.
- **Model Selector**: A compact, pill-shaped model selector directly above the input bar.
- **Multi-Output**: Support generating 4 variations at once, automatically arranged in a 2x2 grid on the canvas.

## 3. Advanced Node Types
Beyond simple image cards, introduce:
- **Comparison Node**: A dual-view card for side-by-side comparison of "Before" and "After" (e.g., for upscaling or inpainting).
- **Control Node**: A card containing sliders for `CFG Scale`, `Steps`, and `Denoising Strength` that connects to and affects subsequent generation calls.

## 4. Project Management & Dashboard
Move from a single global canvas to multiple "Projects".

### Implementation:
- **Project Sidebar**: A collapsible left sidebar (RunningHub style) to switch between different creative sessions.
- **Auto-Snapshot**: Save the entire canvas state (zoom, pan, cards, chat) every 30 seconds to IndexedDB.
- **Project Metadata**: Track creation date, model used, and a thumbnail for each project.

## 5. UI/UX Polishing
- **Emerald Theme**: Adopt the RunningHub emerald/neon accent colors for buttons and progress bars.
- **Canvas Gestures**: 
  - `Space + Drag` for panning.
  - `Middle Click` for quick zoom reset.
  - `Box Selection`: Shift + Drag to select and move multiple cards.
